## Output contract (brief bullet-list mode)

Output only a short plain text bullet list:

- Each item must start with a dash (`-`) followed by a space.
- Each item must be on its own line and hold one short fact or event.
- Do not add numbering, paragraphs, or additional formatting.
- Do not include any text outside of the list.
- The whole list must stay well under a quarter of the input length: at most 8 items and at
  most 18 words per item. Merge related facts and omit minor details.
