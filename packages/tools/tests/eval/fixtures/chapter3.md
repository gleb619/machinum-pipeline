# Chapter 3 — Frostbloom

Silverthorn walked across Oakbridge at first light. The Silverthorn clan had guarded Frostbloom for centuries, and the old oaths were carved into every beam of the bridge.

Now the Ember Key was lost. Without the Ember Key, the light beneath Frostbloom would fade, and the valley would forget the names of its dead.

Thorn stopped at the center of the bridge and looked back. Somewhere behind her, the last keeper of Frostbloom was waking to an empty hall.
