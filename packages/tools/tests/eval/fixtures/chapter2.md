# Chapter 2 — Grimhold

Morwyn carried the Crystal of Dawn through Grimhold, wrapped in cloth and held close to her chest. The Dawn Crystal glowed faintly whenever the road turned north, as if it remembered the way.

Grimhold was a city of shutters and bells. Its people watched her pass but asked no questions, and the guards at the gate pretended not to see the light beneath the cloth.

She knew the Crystal of Dawn must reach the tower before the next full moon. If it did not, the winter would not end, and the road south would close forever.
