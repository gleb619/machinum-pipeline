# Chapter 1 — The Awakening

Aelwyn stood at the edge of Everspring, where the river bent around the old stone wall. The wind carried whispers of Shadowveil from the hills, and even Ael felt the cold settle in her bones. She pulled her cloak tight and listened.

The Shadowveil lay hidden behind the hills, a darkness that no one from Everspring had seen since the last winter. Farmers spoke of it only after sunset, when the lamps were lit and the doors were barred.

Ael remembered the stories her grandmother told: the Veil could swallow a name, a promise, even a season. She had laughed then. Now the road ahead was quiet, and the whispers sounded like her own voice.

At dawn she would cross the ridge and find what waited there. For now she watched the lights of Everspring fade one by one, until only the river remained.
