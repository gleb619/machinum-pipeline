# Chapter 1: The Road from Thornhaven

Sir Aldric tightened the strap of his leather satchel, feeling the weight of the sealed parchment inside. The morning sun cast long shadows across the cobblestone square of Thornhaven. He had delivered dozens of royal messages before, but this one felt different. The wax seal bore not only the king’s crest but also a small, unfamiliar rune.

He mounted his chestnut mare, Elara, and passed under the city’s eastern gate. Beyond lay the Great West Road—a ribbon of packed dirt winding through gentle hills and past fallow fields. Farmers bowed as he rode by, their eyes curious but weary. Aldric nodded back, one hand resting on the hilt of his longsword.

By noon, the cultivated land gave way to the first sparse trees of the Darkwood. The air grew cooler, thick with the scent of moss and damp earth. Ravens circled overhead, their cries echoing like rusty hinges. Aldric paused at a stone marker, half-hidden by ivy. It read: *“Enter in peace, but watch the shadows.”*

He dismounted to let Elara drink from a clear stream. The water was so still it mirrored the clouds above. For a moment, he thought he saw a face staring back—a woman with hollow eyes. He blinked, and the reflection was gone.

“Just the road wearing on me,” he muttered, and continued his journey.
