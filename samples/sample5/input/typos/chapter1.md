# Chapter 1: The Road from Thornhaven

Sir Aldric tightnd the strap of his leather satchel, feeling the wieght of the sealed parchment inside. The morning sun cast long shadows accross the cobblestone square of Thornhaven . He had delivered dozens of royal messages befor, but this one felt diferent. The wax seal bore not only the kings crest but also a small, unfamilar rune.

He mounted his chestnut mare, Elara, and pased under the city's eastern gate. Beyond lay the Great West Road—a ribbon of packed dirt winding through gentle hills and past fallow feilds. Farmers bowed as he rode by,their eyes curious but weary. Aldric nodded back, one hand resting on the hilt of his longsword.
