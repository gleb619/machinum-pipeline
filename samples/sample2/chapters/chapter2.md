[ru]# Chapter 2: Ambush in the Darkwood[/ru]

[ru]The trees closed in like witnesses. Aldric drew his sword.[/ru]

[ru]A branch snapped to his left. Then another to his right.[/ru]

[ru]"Halt, courier," a voice growled from the gloom. "Leave the purse and the letter. You keep your life."[/ru]

[ru]Aldric didn't answer. He slid from the saddle, shield up. Three men stepped from behind the oaks—ragged, armed with rusted axes and a notched longbow. Their leader wore a patch over one eye and grinned with broken teeth.[/ru]

[ru]"I said—"[/ru]

[ru]Aldric charged.[/ru]

[ru]The first brigand swung wide. Aldric ducked, drove his shoulder into the man's chest, and sent him crashing into a root. The second raised his axe. Too slow. Aldric's pommel struck his temple with a dull crack. The man crumpled.[/ru]

[ru]*Thrum.* An arrow grazed Aldric's pauldron.[/ru]

[ru]The one-eyed leader dropped the bow and drew a short blade. He lunged. Aldric sidestepped, swept the man's leg, and pressed his sword against the brigand's throat.[/ru]

[ru]"You were saying?"[/ru]

[ru]The leader spat blood. "Finish it."[/ru]

[ru]Aldric paused. Then he lowered his blade, bound the three men with rope from his saddle, and left them tied to an oak.[/ru]

[ru]"A magistrate will find you by dusk," he said, and rode deeper into the wood.
