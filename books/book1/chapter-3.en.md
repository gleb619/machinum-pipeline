# Chapter 3: The Duke’s Tribunal

Upon the third day, Sir Aldric arrived at the fortress of Blackwater Reach. The keep stood upon a promontory of grey stone, its towers wreathed in low morning mist. He presented his credentials to the gate warden and was admitted without delay.

The great hall was sparsely occupied. Duke Valerius sat upon a raised dais, flanked by two advisors in crimson robes. A scribe stood at a lectern to the duke’s right. The air smelled of beeswax and old parchment.

“Sir Aldric of Thornhaven,” the duke intoned. “You have travelled far. What news from His Majesty?”

Aldric knelt and removed the sealed parchment from his satchel. “Your Grace, I am commanded to deliver this message directly into your hands. I have not read its contents, nor has any other.”

A steward carried the letter to the duke. Valerius broke the seal, read in silence, then nodded once.

“The king requests my presence at the autumn council,” he said. “And he commends your conduct in the Darkwood. The brigands you bound testified this morning.”

Aldric remained kneeling.

“Rise, sir,” the duke continued. “You have upheld the courier’s oath. Take rest in the south guest chamber. Tomorrow, you shall carry my reply.”

“Thank you, Your Grace,” Aldric replied.

He bowed and withdrew. The heavy oaken doors closed behind him. Outside, the wind carried the scent of distant rain and the low thunder of a coming storm. The journey was complete. The oath was honoured.
