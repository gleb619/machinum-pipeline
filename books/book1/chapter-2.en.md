# Chapter 2: Ambush in the Darkwood

The trees closed in like witnesses. Aldric drew his sword.

A branch snapped to his left. Then another to his right.

"Halt, courier," a voice growled from the gloom. "Leave the purse and the letter. You keep your life."

Aldric didn't answer. He slid from the saddle, shield up. Three men stepped from behind the oaks—ragged, armed with rusted axes and a notched longbow. Their leader wore a patch over one eye and grinned with broken teeth.

"I said—"

Aldric charged.

The first brigand swung wide. Aldric ducked, drove his shoulder into the man's chest, and sent him crashing into a root. The second raised his axe. Too slow. Aldric's pommel struck his temple with a dull crack. The man crumpled.

*Thrum.* An arrow grazed Aldric's pauldron.

The one-eyed leader dropped the bow and drew a short blade. He lunged. Aldric sidestepped, swept the man's leg, and pressed his sword against the brigand's throat.

"You were saying?"

The leader spat blood. "Finish it."

Aldric paused. Then he lowered his blade, bound the three men with rope from his saddle, and left them tied to an oak.

"A magistrate will find you by dusk," he said, and rode deeper into the wood.
